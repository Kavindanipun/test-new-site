GLASS & CASE / HELP & SUPPORT

Frequently
Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about finding your perfect fit.

What types of tempered glass do you offer?

We offer MTB, Super D, Privacy and Mossily tempered glass options. Contact us with your phone model and we’ll help you choose the right one.

How do I find the right glass for my phone?

Share your exact phone brand and model with us on WhatsApp. We can help you check available compatible options before you order.

What is Privacy tempered glass?

Privacy tempered glass is designed to make your screen harder to view from the sides while keeping it readable from directly in front. The viewing effect may vary by model and lighting.

Does tempered glass protect my screen from breaking?

Tempered glass adds a sacrificial protective layer against scratches and some impacts. It cannot guarantee that the original screen will survive every drop or impact.

How can I order tempered glass or a back cover?

Tap Explore Tempered Glass or Explore Back Covers, or use Contact at the top of the site to message us on WhatsApp. Include your phone model and the product you want.
